About

I am a master's student at the University of Michigan School of Information. Before that, I received my B.A. from Tsinghua University, Academy of Arts and Design.

My work is centered on robot learning and embodied AI, especially humanoid reinforcement learning, human-to-robot data conversion, VLA policy learning, and real-robot deployment. Recently, I have worked on Unitree G1 soccer motion policies, PrimeU simulation and retargeting pipelines, and human-to-humanoid supervision for high-DoF VLA policy training.

Research Interests

  • Humanoid reinforcement learning and sim-to-real control. Motion capture, retargeting, policy training, ONNX deployment, and real-robot validation for humanoid motion.
  • Human-to-humanoid data conversion. Ego-exo video pipelines, action retargeting, and executable high-DoF supervision for humanoid VLA training.
  • Spatial rewards for VLA policies. Offline RL with spatial representation rewards for sparse-reward manipulation tasks under limited compute.
  • Evaluation and data construction. Simulation rollout construction, failure data, leak-safe validation, and benchmark-driven iteration.
